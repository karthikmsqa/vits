Study & settle

Student life, made a little easier.

A Victoria-focused starter guide for UVic, Camosun and other new students: everyday food, safe housing, work rules, support and community.

Start with your campus

Your first month checklist

Confirm your school’s international student services

Check orientation, immigration advising, health insurance and student supports. Use school advisers for your specific circumstances.

Make a realistic budget

Plan for rent, deposits, groceries, phone, transportation, textbooks and winter clothing.

Understand employment limits

Verify your study permit conditions and current IRCC rules before taking an off-campus or part-time job.

Learn transit routes before signing a lease

Test commute time to campus, evening bus frequency and nearby grocery stores.

Connect with people

Look for student clubs, campus counselling, peer support and cultural/community events.

Eat well, spend wisely

Groceries, restaurants & daily life

🛒

Everyday groceries

Compare weekly flyers at Save-On-Foods, Thrifty Foods, Fairway Market, Walmart, Costco and smaller neighborhood grocers. Inventory and student pricing vary; check the closest location and transit route.

🌶️

South Asian ingredients

Search for Indian/South Asian grocery stores in Victoria and Saanich for rice varieties, dals, spices, curry leaves and frozen staples. Call ahead for specialty Tamil ingredients, as stock changes frequently.

View nearby grocery options ↗
🍛

Food & restaurants

Victoria has Indian and South Asian restaurants alongside many other cuisines. For dosa, idli, biryani or vegetarian menus, check recent menus and availability before traveling.

You are not alone

Support, learning & belonging

Student & mental health support

Your campus can direct you to counselling, accessibility services, student food supports, peer mentoring and academic assistance. For health advice call 811; for emergencies call 911.

Part-time jobs & volunteering

Start with campus career boards, WorkBC and reputable local employers. Never pay upfront for a job offer. Confirm study-permit work conditions and applicable employment standards.
