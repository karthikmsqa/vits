About VITS

Rooted in Tamil. At home on the Island.

A community-focused space to share language, strengthen friendships and celebrate Tamil traditions in Victoria and across Vancouver Island.

Who we are

A bridge between cultures and generations.

Vancouver Island Tamil Sangam (VITS) aims to bring Tamil families, students, professionals and friends together in a warm, inclusive environment. We celebrate the richness of Tamil art, food, language and heritage while embracing the multicultural communities and Indigenous histories of this island.

Our vision is simple: help newcomers feel connected, give young people opportunities to learn their heritage, and build lasting friendships across communities.
