About VITS

Building belonging through culture and community.

VITS is a welcoming community platform for Tamil families, youth, students, newcomers, and friends of Tamil culture across Greater Victoria and Vancouver Island.

Community · Culture · Belonging

Vision

A visible and connected Tamil community rooted in heritage and open to the broader island community.

Mission

Support cultural continuity, practical connection, and intergenerational learning.

Style

Family-friendly, welcoming, volunteer-driven, and locally connected.

Place

Victoria and Vancouver Island, with room for local partnerships and celebrations.

Core services

What VITS can organize and support.

📘

Weekly Tamil lessons

Language exposure, reading, conversation, and cultural connection for younger generations.

🎉

Annual gatherings

Pongal, Tamil Heritage Month, family socials, and shared cultural celebrations.

🏕️

Camp, picnic & outings

Community picnics, island day trips, and family-friendly outdoor gatherings.

🤝

Volunteer & collaborate

Opportunities to participate in events, support newcomers, or contribute ideas.

🌿

Youth & family connection

Space for children and youth to experience Tamil identity in a Canadian setting.

🎭

Cultural participation

Music, dance, literature, food, and heritage programming that others can join and appreciate.

Why it matters

An Indo-Canadian bridge on Vancouver Island.

For many families, culture feels strongest when it is visible in daily life. VITS helps Tamil traditions stay alive while encouraging local integration, friendship, and contribution.

  • Celebrate identity without isolation.
  • Help newcomers feel less alone.
  • Create shared experiences for children and youth.
  • Invite the wider community to learn about Tamil heritage.
