New to Victoria?

Your practical guide to settling in.

A Victoria-focused newcomer guide. Always verify eligibility and current requirements at the official sources.

Essential resources

Settle in, one step at a time.

🪪

Apply for SIN

A Social Insurance Number is required for work and certain benefits. Apply through Service Canada; keep it confidential.

Visit official resource ↗
🏥

MSP health coverage

Register for BC Medical Services Plan if eligible; check your waiting period and interim insurance.

Visit official resource ↗
🩺

Health: 811, urgent care and 911

Call 811 for non-emergency health advice; use urgent and primary care for prompt non-life-threatening concerns. Call 911 or visit an ER for emergencies.

Visit official resource ↗
🏠

Renting in BC

Read the lease, document move-in condition, and know your rights. Security deposits are generally capped at half a month’s rent for covered tenancies.

Visit official resource ↗
📱

Phones and banking

Compare prepaid and postpaid phone plans, coverage and international calling; compare banking account fees and newcomer offers.

Visit official resource ↗
🚌

Victoria buses

BC Transit serves Victoria, Saanich, UVic, Camosun, Sidney and the ferry terminal. Plan routes and compare fares and passes.

Visit official resource ↗
⛴️

Ferries and travel

Swartz Bay connects with Tsawwassen near Vancouver. Check schedules, reservations and terminal check-in requirements.

Visit official resource ↗
✈️

Victoria airport and taxis

Victoria International Airport is at YYJ near Sidney. Compare transit, taxis, shuttles and rideshare before arriving.

Visit official resource ↗
🚘

Driving licence and lessons

New BC residents may need to exchange licences or take tests. Review ICBC rules and look for an ICBC-recognized driving school.

Visit official resource ↗
💼

Find work and build your resume

Explore WorkBC job-search services, nearby WorkBC Centres and community library resources. Verify work authorization.

Visit official resource ↗
📚

Libraries and recreation

GVPL has books, digital resources and study facilities. Municipal rec centres offer swimming, fitness and family activities.

Visit official resource ↗
🤝

Local immigrant support

The Inter-Cultural Association of Greater Victoria and VIRCS help eligible newcomers with settlement and referrals.

Visit official resource ↗
🇮🇳

Indian passport renewal

Use the official Consulate General of India in Vancouver for current passport renewal, application provider and appointment requirements.

Visit official resource ↗
🪔

Victoria Hindu Temple

Victoria Hindu Mandir: 1934 Cultra Avenue, Saanichton. Contact the temple for current worship and festival timings.

Visit official resource ↗
