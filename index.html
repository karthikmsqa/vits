Our mission

A little bit of home, a lot of belonging.

VITS is envisioned as a gathering place for Tamil-speaking people and everyone curious about Tamil culture across Greater Victoria and Vancouver Island.

Through language learning, cultural celebrations and warm community connections, we can carry our traditions forward while participating fully in the diverse life of British Columbia.
