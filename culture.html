Tamil heritage

A living heritage, shared across generations.

From the rhythm of Bharatanatyam to the warmth of Pongal, discover how Tamil traditions enrich Vancouver Island’s multicultural life.

January · Tamil Heritage Month

Our history is part of Canada’s story.

Every January, Canada recognizes Tamil Heritage Month, an opportunity to honour the language, history, arts and contributions of Tamil Canadians. The House of Commons recognized the month in 2016, alongside the cherished harvest celebration of Thai Pongal.

On Vancouver Island, heritage is lived in shared meals, Tamil classes, intergenerational storytelling, dance, music and participation in local cultural events. Celebrating our traditions can also deepen friendships with neighbours of all backgrounds.

We recognize that Greater Victoria is on the territories of Indigenous Peoples, including the lək̓ʷəŋən (Lekwungen) peoples and the W̱SÁNEĆ peoples. We encourage learning from and respecting local Nations.
